Lettertrail Support

Need help with Lettertrail, found a bug, or is a word missing? Write to pouyan.meishany@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone you use and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Common questions

How do I play?
Guess the hidden word in six tries. After each try the letters turn green (right letter, right place), yellow (right letter, wrong place) or grey (not in the word). Tap the ⓘ on the Home screen for the full rules.

The game says my word is not a word.
The dictionary is large but not complete. Words the game refuses are reviewed and real ones are added in later updates.

I lost my progress.
Progress is kept on your phone only. Deleting the game deletes it, and it does not move to a new phone.

Online play does not find a match.
Online play needs an internet connection. If no other player is waiting, you are matched with a computer player after a short wait.

How do I get the newest version?
Small updates download by themselves. Close the game completely and open it again, twice, to pick one up. Larger updates come through the App Store.

How do I stop the game sending gameplay data?
Open the ⓘ popup on the Home screen and turn off "Share anonymous gameplay data". See the privacy policy for details.
