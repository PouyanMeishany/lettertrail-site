Lettertrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Lettertrail is a word game made by Pouyan Meishany. This page says what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • You play without an account. The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • There are no ads and no advertising or tracking tools from other companies in the game.
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay data to the developer's own server, to keep online play working and to make the game better. You can switch the optional part off.

What the game collects

A random install number. The first time the game needs the server, it makes up a random identifier and keeps it on your device. It is not tied to your name, your Apple account or your device's own identifiers. Deleting the game deletes it; a new install gets a new one.

Results. When you finish a level, the Daily Word or a daily chain, the game sends the result (for example the level, the number of tries, the points) with the install number, so the game can show how you compare with other players.

Online matches. When you play an online match, the words you play, your points and your turns are sent to the server so the match can run. Other players see a randomly chosen nickname, never anything about you.

Words the dictionary refused. When you type a word the game does not know, the word may be sent to the server so that missing words can be added.

Anonymous gameplay data (optional). Unless you switch it off, the game sends events such as "level started", "level finished", "hint used" and "app opened", with small numbers (level, tries, seconds) and the app version. If the game hits an error, the first 200 characters of the error message are sent. These events never contain a word you typed, a name or free text.

Technical data. Like any internet service, the server sees your IP address while it answers a request. It is used to deliver the service and to limit abuse, and it is not stored with your gameplay data.

What the game does not collect

Your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, purchases, advertising identifier or browsing history.

Why it is collected

  • to run online matches and daily results;
  • to find levels that are too hard, broken or boring, and fix them;
  • to add missing words to the dictionary;
  • to find and fix errors.

It is never used for advertising and never sold or shared with anyone for their own use.

Where it is kept and for how long

The data is kept on a server run by the developer. Gameplay events are deleted after about 400 days. Results and matches are kept while the game is in service.

Your choices

  • Switch off the optional data. Open the "How to play" (ⓘ) popup on the Home screen and turn off "Share anonymous gameplay data". The game then stops sending those events and clears any that were waiting to be sent.
  • Delete everything on your device. Deleting the game removes your progress and your install number from the device.
  • Ask for deletion or a copy. Write to the address below. Because the game does not know who you are, please say roughly when you played so the data can be found; where it cannot be linked to you, it stays anonymous.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no chat, no profiles and no purchases.

Pictures in the game

The portraits and landmark pictures on the collection cards are public-domain or CC0 works from Wikimedia Commons. Maps are drawn from Natural Earth data.
